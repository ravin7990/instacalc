How the NPS Calculator Projects Pension & Retirement Wealth

The National Pension System (NPS) is a voluntary, long-term retirement savings scheme regulated by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA). Our NPS calculator models corpus accumulation during your working career, estimating your final retirement wealth, the 60% tax-free lump sum withdrawal, and the recurring monthly pension generated by the mandatory 40% annuity.

The 60% Tax-Free Lumpsum & 40% Mandatory Annuity Framework

Upon attaining 60 years of age, NPS subscribers are permitted to withdraw up to 60% of their accumulated retirement corpus as a 100% tax-free lump sum. The remaining minimum 40% of the corpus must be utilized to purchase an annuity from a PFRDA-registered Annuity Service Provider (such as LIC, HDFC Life, or SBI Life) to yield guaranteed monthly pension for life.

Exclusive Tax Deductions: Section 80CCD(1B) ₹50,000 Benefit

Under the Old Tax Regime, NPS Tier-1 accounts offer an exclusive tax deduction of up to ₹50,000 under Section 80CCD(1B), over and above the ₹1.5 Lakh limit of Section 80C. Additionally, employer contributions up to 10% of salary (Basic + DA) or 14% for Central/State Government employees qualify for deduction under Section 80CCD(2) under both tax regimes.

Active Choice vs Auto Choice: Designing Your Asset Allocation

NPS gives investors investment flexibility through two primary approaches. Under Active Choice, you decide your allocation across Equity (Asset Class E, up to 75%), Corporate Bonds (Asset Class C), and Government Securities (Asset Class G). Under Auto Choice, lifecycle funds automatically reduce your equity exposure as you age to preserve capital.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the National Pension System (NPS) work upon reaching age 60?

Upon reaching 60 years of age, NPS subscribers can withdraw up to 60% of their accumulated retirement corpus as a 100% tax-free lump sum. The remaining minimum 40% must be utilized to purchase an annuity from a PFRDA-approved Annuity Service Provider to yield regular monthly pension.

What additional tax deductions are available exclusively for NPS Tier-1 accounts?

Under Section 80CCD(1B), individuals can claim an exclusive tax deduction of up to ₹50,000 for NPS Tier-1 contributions over and above the Section 80C ₹1.5 Lakh limit under the Old Tax Regime. Corporate employer contributions qualify under Section 80CCD(2).

What asset classes are available for investment within NPS?

NPS allocates funds across four asset classes: Equity (E, up to 75%), Corporate Bonds (C), Government Securities (G), and Alternative Investment Funds (A, up to 5%). Subscribers can select Active Choice (custom allocation) or Auto Choice (lifecycle funds adjusted by age).

Is the monthly annuity pension received from NPS taxable in India?

Yes. While purchasing the annuity with 40% of the corpus is tax-free, the recurring monthly pension income generated by the annuity plan is treated as salary/income and taxed at your applicable income tax slab rate in the financial year received.
