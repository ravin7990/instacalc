Understanding Continuous Time Duration & Interval Math

Calculating the exact elapsed duration between two points in time is crucial for event planning, flight schedules, multi-day work shifts, server uptime monitoring, and athletic race timing. Unlike simple time-of-day differences, continuous duration calculations account for calendar day rollovers and variable month lengths.


Decomposition of Total Duration:

  • Total Seconds: $\text{End Time (ms)} - \text{Start Time (ms)} \div 1000$
  • Decomposed Format: Decomposes into Days ($D = \lfloor\frac{S}{86400}\rfloor$), Hours ($H = \lfloor\frac{S \bmod 86400}{3600}\rfloor$), Minutes ($M = \lfloor\frac{S \bmod 3600}{60}\rfloor$), and Seconds ($S \bmod 60$).
  • Decimal Equivalents: Useful for engineering and billing conversions (e.g. 1 day 12 hours = 1.50 days or 36.00 hours).
How do you calculate exact elapsed time between two timestamps?

The calculator converts both start and end timestamps into UTC milliseconds, computes the difference, and decomposes the result into days, hours, minutes, and seconds.

Can I add or subtract duration from a starting timestamp?

Yes. Use the "Add / Subtract Duration" tab to add or subtract days, hours, minutes, and seconds from any date and time.

Does this tool handle daylight saving time changes?

Calculations use browser-native high-precision timestamps, ensuring exact linear elapsed seconds between dates.

Can I copy or export the duration summary?

Yes. Click the "Copy Duration Summary" button to quickly copy the decomposed duration, total hours, and total seconds to your clipboard.
