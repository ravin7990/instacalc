How the Week Calculator Computes Weeks and Days Between Dates

Our week calculator computes the exact number of weeks and remaining days between two dates. Ideal for academic term planning, project roadmap milestones, pregnancy gestation tracking, and rental lease periods, the calculator outputs both fractional decimal weeks and human-readable week and day combinations.

The International ISO 8601 Week Standard Explained

Under the international ISO 8601 standard, calendar weeks commence on Monday, and Week 1 of any calendar year is defined as the week containing the first Thursday of that year (or containing January 4th). Our calculator displays the current active ISO week number alongside total weeks elapsed.

Agile Scrum Sprint Planning & Quarterly Roadmap Increments

Software engineering teams running 2-week or 3-week Agile Scrum sprints use the week calculator to plan release cadences. Inputting the project kickoff date and release deadline reveals the exact number of sprint iterations available to allocate engineering backlog stories.

Tracking Gestational Pregnancy Weeks from Last Menstrual Period (LMP)

Obstetricians measure pregnancy gestation in completed weeks starting from the first day of the mother's Last Menstrual Period (LMP). Expectant parents can select their LMP date to determine current gestational age, completed trimesters, and the countdown to the standard 40-week due date.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the week calculator compute weeks between two dates?

The week calculator calculates total elapsed calendar days between two dates and divides by 7: Weeks = Total Days / 7. It outputs both fractional decimal weeks (e.g., 5.43 weeks) and standard human-readable format: 5 weeks and 3 days.

What is an ISO 8601 week number and how is it determined?

Under the international ISO 8601 standard, a week starts on Monday, and Week 1 of any calendar year is defined as the week that contains the first Thursday of that year (or the week that includes January 4th). A calendar year consists of either 52 or 53 ISO weeks.

How do Agile software teams utilize the week calculator for Scrum sprint planning?

Software engineering teams running 2-week or 3-week sprint cycles enter their project release milestone date and sprint commencement date to compute exact sprint iterations and allocate deliverables across quarterly roadmap increments.

Can this tool calculate gestational pregnancy weeks from the last menstrual period (LMP)?

Yes. By selecting the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP) as the start date and today as the end date, the calculator indicates your exact gestational age in completed weeks and days towards the standard 40-week term.
