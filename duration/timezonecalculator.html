Navigating Global Time Zones & Meeting Scheduling

The Earth is divided into 24 standard time zones based on 15-degree longitudinal increments around the globe, with Greenwich, London serving as the prime meridian (0° longitude, UTC+0). However, geopolitical boundaries, half-hour offsets (such as India's UTC+5:30 and Adelaide's UTC+9:30), and seasonal Daylight Saving Time (DST) adjustments make manual conversion prone to errors.


Overcoming Meeting Overlap Challenges: Coordinating remote teams across continents requires finding overlapping business hours. Our converter automatically highlights whether the calculated time falls within normal working hours (9:00 AM – 6:00 PM), evening hours, or overnight sleeping hours in the recipient's city.


Standard Global Time References:

  • UTC / GMT: Coordinated Universal Time (London, UK)
  • EST / EDT: US Eastern Time (New York, Washington DC, Toronto) — UTC-5 / UTC-4
  • PST / PDT: US Pacific Time (San Francisco, Los Angeles, Seattle) — UTC-8 / UTC-7
  • IST: India Standard Time (Mumbai, New Delhi, Bengaluru) — UTC+5:30 (no DST)
  • JST / KST: Japan & Korea Standard Time (Tokyo, Seoul) — UTC+9 (no DST)
Why does India (IST) have a 30-minute fractional offset?

India established a single national time zone at UTC+5:30 based on the central meridian passing through Mirzapur (82.5° East longitude) to balance daylight distribution across the entire subcontinent.

What is the International Date Line (IDL)?

The International Date Line lies roughly along 180° longitude in the Pacific Ocean. Crossing the line westbound adds a full calendar day (+24 hours), while crossing eastbound subtracts a day (-24 hours).

How can I plan cross-timezone meetings without disturbing colleagues?

Use the "Working Hours Match" status indicator in this tool. If an overlap window is narrow, schedule recurring meetings alternately to share the burden of early or late calls fairly.

Are Australian time zones subject to daylight saving?

Southern and eastern states (Sydney, Melbourne, Adelaide) observe DST (shifting from AEST UTC+10 to AEDT UTC+11), while Queensland, Northern Territory, and Western Australia do not observe DST.
