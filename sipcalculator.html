How this SIP return calculator works

This SIP investment calculator online computes corpus month by month. It applies contribution timing, monthly compounding, annual step-up amounts, and target planning to produce an accurate SIP maturity amount and wealth gain projection over your selected time horizon.


Formula reference (without step-up): FV = P * (((1 + r)^n - 1) / r) * (1 + r), where P is monthly SIP, r is monthly rate, and n is total months.

Is SIP return guaranteed in India?

No. SIP returns depend on market-linked mutual fund performance. This tool gives projections based on your expected annual return assumptions.

What is step up SIP and why is it important?

Step-up SIP increases your monthly investment yearly by a selected percentage. It is one of the most effective ways to reach large corpus targets faster.

How to calculate SIP returns for long-term goals?

Enter your monthly SIP amount, expected return, and tenure in the SIP calculator. It compounds contributions monthly and estimates your total invested amount and maturity corpus.

What does inflation adjusted SIP corpus mean?

Inflation-adjusted corpus estimates what your future SIP maturity amount will be worth in today's purchasing power, helping you plan realistic financial goals.

Can NRI invest in SIP in India?

Yes, NRIs can invest in SIPs through eligible KYC-compliant NRO or NRE accounts, subject to fund house and regulatory rules.

How is required SIP for target calculated?

The calculator uses iterative estimation to find the exact starting monthly SIP amount needed to reach your specific goal corpus under your return assumptions.

What is a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)?

SIP is a method of investing a fixed amount regularly in a mutual fund scheme. It instills financial discipline and leverages rupee-cost averaging to mitigate market timing risks.

How much minimum amount is required to start a SIP?

You can start a SIP with as little as ₹500 per month in most mutual fund schemes in India, making it a highly accessible investment vehicle.

Can I skip a SIP installment?

Yes, you can pause or skip a SIP installment if you run short on funds. Your mutual fund house will not charge a penalty, though your bank might charge a mandate bounce fee.

Can I withdraw my money from a SIP at any time?

In open-ended mutual funds, you can withdraw your SIP corpus at any time. However, ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) funds have a mandatory lock-in period of 3 years per installment.

Which date is best for a SIP installment?

There is no 'best' date for a SIP that guarantees higher returns. It is recommended to set your SIP date a few days after your salary is credited (e.g., between the 1st and 5th of the month).

Is SIP better than a Recurring Deposit (RD)?

Historically, equity mutual fund SIPs have offered inflation-beating returns over the long term compared to RDs, though they involve market risk. RDs offer fixed interest but are taxed at your slab rate.

How are SIP returns taxed in India?

For equity mutual funds, short-term capital gains (less than 1 year) are taxed at 20%. Long-term capital gains (more than 1 year) over ₹1.25 lakh in a financial year are taxed at 12.5%.

How long should I continue my SIP?

Ideally, you should link your SIPs to specific financial goals such as retirement or a child's education, and continue investing for a long-term horizon (7-10+ years) to best leverage the power of compounding.
