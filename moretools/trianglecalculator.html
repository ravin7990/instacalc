How the Triangle Calculator Solves Sides, Angles, Area & Perimeter

Our triangle calculator solves unknown sides, interior angles, surface area, and perimeter for all triangle types: right-angled, isosceles, equilateral, acute, and obtuse scalene triangles. Enter any three known values (including at least one side) to compute complete geometric solutions instantly.

Heron's Formula for SSS Triangles: Semi-Perimeter Area Calculation

When all three sides (a, b, c) of a triangle are known, calculate area using Heron's formula without needing the height. First find the semi-perimeter: s = (a + b + c) / 2. Then calculate Area = √[s * (s - a) * (s - b) * (s - c)]. Interior angles are then resolved using the Law of Cosines.

The Law of Sines and Law of Cosines for Oblique Scalene Triangles

For triangles without a 90-degree right angle, use the Law of Sines: a / sin(A) = b / sin(B) = c / sin(C) when you know two angles and one side (AAS/ASA). Use the Law of Cosines: c² = a² + b² - (2ab * cos(C)) when two sides and the included angle (SAS) are known, or when finding angles from three sides (SSS).

The Triangle Inequality Theorem: Essential Geometric Constraints

For any three line segments to form a valid triangle, the Triangle Inequality Theorem mandates that the sum of the lengths of any two sides must be strictly greater than the third side: a + b > c, a + c > b, and b + c > a. If the sum equals or is less than the third side, no triangle can physically exist.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the triangle calculator solve a triangle with 3 known sides (SSS)?

When all 3 sides (a, b, c) are known, the calculator first verifies the Triangle Inequality Theorem (a + b > c). It then computes the semi-perimeter s = (a + b + c) / 2 and calculates the area using Heron's formula: Area = √[s * (s - a) * (s - b) * (s - c)]. Interior angles are resolved using the Law of Cosines.

What is the difference between solving right, equilateral, and scalene triangles?

A right triangle has one 90° angle and can be solved directly using the Pythagorean theorem (a² + b² = c²). An equilateral triangle has three equal 60° angles and sides. A scalene triangle has three unequal sides and angles, requiring the Law of Sines or Law of Cosines to solve.

What is the Law of Sines and Law of Cosines?

The Law of Sines states that the ratio of each side to the sine of its opposite angle is constant: a / sin(A) = b / sin(B) = c / sin(C). The Law of Cosines relates all three sides to one angle: c² = a² + b² - (2ab * cos(C)), ideal when two sides and the included angle (SAS) are known.

Why must the sum of any two sides of a triangle be greater than the third side?

The Triangle Inequality Theorem dictates that the shortest distance between two vertices is a straight line. If the sum of any two sides were equal to or less than the third side, the vertices would collapse into a flat straight line or fail to connect, forming no triangle.
