How the Trigonometry Calculator Evaluates Primary & Reciprocal Ratios

Our trigonometry calculator evaluates the six standard trigonometric functions: Sine (sin), Cosine (cos), Tangent (tan), Cosecant (csc), Secant (sec), and Cotangent (cot). It also computes inverse trigonometric functions (arcsin, arccos, arctan) across both degree (DEG) and radian (RAD) angular modes.

SOH-CAH-TOA: Right Triangle Geometric Definitions Explained

In right-angled triangle geometry, the fundamental ratios are remembered through the mnemonic SOH-CAH-TOA: Sine = Opposite / Hypotenuse (SOH); Cosine = Adjacent / Hypotenuse (CAH); and Tangent = Opposite / Adjacent (TOA). The reciprocal functions are Cosecant (1/sin), Secant (1/cos), and Cotangent (1/tan).

Converting Between Degrees and Radians (π Radians = 180°)

Angular measurement uses degrees (full circle = 360°) and radians (full circle = 2π radians). To convert degrees to radians, multiply by π and divide by 180: Radians = Degrees * (π / 180). To convert radians to degrees, multiply by 180 and divide by π: Degrees = Radians * (180 / π). For example, 45° = π/4 radians (~0.7854 rad).

Fundamental Pythagorean Identities & Inverse Trigonometric Functions

The primary Pythagorean trigonometric identity states: sin²(θ) + cos²(θ) = 1. Dividing by cos²(θ) yields tan²(θ) + 1 = sec²(θ), while dividing by sin²(θ) gives 1 + cot²(θ) = csc²(θ). Inverse trigonometric functions (arcsin, arccos, arctan) determine the unknown angle θ when side ratio values are known.

Frequently Asked Questions

What trigonometric functions does this calculator support?

The calculator evaluates the six standard trigonometric functions: Sine (sin), Cosine (cos), Tangent (tan), Cosecant (csc), Secant (sec), and Cotangent (cot). It also computes their inverse functions (arcsin, arccos, arctan) in both degrees and radians.

What is the mnemonic SOH-CAH-TOA and what does it mean?

SOH-CAH-TOA is a fundamental trigonometry mnemonic for right-angled triangles: SOH stands for Sine = Opposite / Hypotenuse; CAH stands for Cosine = Adjacent / Hypotenuse; and TOA stands for Tangent = Opposite / Adjacent.

How do you convert angles between degrees and radians?

To convert degrees to radians, multiply by π and divide by 180: Radians = Degrees * (π / 180). To convert radians to degrees, multiply by 180 and divide by π: Degrees = Radians * (180 / π). For example, 90° = π/2 radians (~1.5708 rad).

What is the fundamental Pythagorean trigonometric identity?

The primary Pythagorean trigonometric identity is: sin²(θ) + cos²(θ) = 1. Derived directly from the unit circle where x = cos(θ) and y = sin(θ), dividing by cos²(θ) yields tan²(θ) + 1 = sec²(θ), and dividing by sin²(θ) yields 1 + cot²(θ) = csc²(θ).
