Account & Data Deletion Request

Life Hack Developer – instacalc.in

Users who have signed in to applications published by developer Life Hack Developer (such as our Age Calculator, LaborBook Pro, Expense Manager, Drawing Viewer (Self Drive), or Bible Verse of the Day) via Google Sign-In (OAuth) or a direct account can request the permanent deletion of their profile and all associated data at any time.

What data we collect

To provide personalized features, saved entries, and calculator tools, we may collect and securely store the following information:

  • Google profile name and email (used exclusively for authentication)
  • Basic profile details: Name, Email, and Password (for direct accounts)
  • App-specific data: Saved dates/events (Age Calculator), labor & attendance entries (LaborBook Pro), income/expense records (Expense Manager)
  • User preferences and personalized app settings
  • Historical calculation logs saved to your profile

How to request deletion

Follow these simple steps to ensure your request is processed quickly:

  • Select the target application from the dropdown menu in the form below.
  • Provide your registered email address (associated with your Google Sign-In or app profile).
  • Choose your desired deletion scope and confirm your request.

What happens next

  • Your request will be prioritized and processed within 7 working days.
  • All personal data and account identifiers will be permanently purged from our production servers.
  • A final confirmation email will be sent once the action is complete.

Data we may retain

After your account is deleted, we only retain anonymous, non-identifiable telemetry (e.g., general usage statistics) that cannot be linked to an individual. This helps us maintain app performance for all users.
