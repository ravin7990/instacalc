How the GST Calculator Computes Inclusive and Exclusive Tax Amounts

The Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a unified indirect consumption tax levied on the supply of goods and services across India. Our GST calculator provides instantaneous dual-mode calculations: adding GST to net base prices (GST Exclusive) or removing GST from gross retail prices to isolate the underlying base cost and tax component (GST Inclusive).

Understanding Intra-State (CGST + SGST) vs Inter-State (IGST) Splits

GST taxation depends on transaction geography. For intra-state transactions (where buyer and seller are in the same state), GST is split equally between Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST) — for example, an 18% GST rate equals 9% CGST + 9% SGST. For inter-state transactions (across state borders), the full 18% is collected as Integrated GST (IGST) by the Central Government.

The Four Standard GST Slabs: 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28% Explained

The GST Council categorizes commodities into four standard tax tiers: 5% (essential goods, packaged food items, and basic apparel), 12% (processed foods, pharmaceuticals, and agricultural machinery), 18% (most manufactured consumer goods, electronics, and IT/software services), and 28% (luxury automobiles, tobacco, and high-end consumer appliances with applicable cess).

Input Tax Credit (ITC) Mechanics for Indian Businesses

Input Tax Credit (ITC) is a foundational mechanism under GST law that prevents the cascading effect of tax-on-tax. Registered businesses can offset the GST paid on commercial purchases, raw materials, capital machinery, and input services against the GST liability due on their outward customer sales, remitting only the net difference to the tax authorities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate inclusive and exclusive GST in India?

To add GST (Exclusive): GST Amount = (Base Price * GST%) / 100, Total = Base + GST. To remove GST (Inclusive): GST Amount = Gross Price - [Gross Price * (100 / (100 + GST%))], Base Price = Gross Price - GST Amount.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

For intra-state sales (within the same state), GST is split equally between Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST) — for example, 18% GST = 9% CGST + 9% SGST. For inter-state sales (between two states), Integrated GST (IGST) at the full 18% rate goes to the Centre.

What are the four primary standard GST rate slabs in India?

The GST Council categorizes goods and services into four primary tax slabs: 5% (essential commodities and packaged food), 12% (processed foods and business services), 18% (most manufactured goods, consumer goods, and IT services), and 28% (luxury items and demerit goods).

What is Input Tax Credit (ITC) under the GST system?

Input Tax Credit (ITC) allows registered businesses to deduct the GST paid on business purchases and input services from the GST liability due on their outward sales, avoiding the cascading effect of double taxation.
