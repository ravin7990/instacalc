Understanding GST Calculations

Our online tools make tax calculations straightforward for both inclusive and exclusive scenarios:


Add GST (Price + GST):
Formula: GST Amount = (Original Price × GST Percentage) / 100
Example: If price is 1,000 and GST is 18%, tax is 180, total is 1,180.


Remove GST (Price inclusive of GST):
Formula: GST Amount = Price - (Price × (100 / (100 + GST Percentage)))
Example: If total bill is 1,180 and tax is 18%, tax is 180, original net price is 1,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GST?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is India's unified indirect tax, effective 1 July 2017, replacing VAT, service tax, and excise for most goods and services.

What are the GST rate slabs?

The main slabs are 0% (essentials), 5% (necessities), 12% and 18% (standard rates), and 28% (luxury and sin goods); special rates apply to gold and certain items.

What is the GST calculation formula?

CGST = value × rate ÷ 2, SGST = value × rate ÷ 2 (intra-state). For inter-state supply, IGST = value × full rate.

How much GST on a ₹10,000 item at 18%?

₹10,000 × 18% = ₹1,800. Intra-state: CGST ₹900 + SGST ₹900. Inter-state: IGST ₹1,800.

Which taxes did GST replace?

GST subsumed VAT, service tax, central excise, central sales tax, entry tax, and several cesses into one tax.

What elements must a GST invoice contain?

GSTIN of supplier and buyer, invoice number, date, HSNSAC codes, taxable value, GST rate and amount, and place of supply.

How do I reverse-calculate GST from a total price?

GST-exclusive value = GST-inclusive price ÷ (1 + rate/100). GST amount = inclusive price − exclusive value.

Does India's GST treat states alike?

GST is a national tax — states receive CGST/SGST shares per constitutional formula, so the rates themselves are uniform nationally.

When is GST registration required?

Businesses over the threshold turnover (₹40 lakh for goods, ₹20 lakh for services; ₹20/₹10 lakh in special-category states) must register.

What is Input Tax Credit (ITC)?

GST paid on purchases (input tax credit) can offset GST collected on sales, so tax is effectively paid only on value added.
