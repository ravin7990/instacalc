What is Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR)?

The Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) is the mean annual growth rate of an investment over a specified period of time longer than one year. It represents the geometric progression ratio that provides a constant rate of return, smoothing out market volatility and showing what an investment yielded on an annually compounded basis.


The Mathematical CAGR Formula

The standard formula is: CAGR = (Ending Value / Beginning Value)^(1 / Years) - 1.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CAGR and how is it calculated?

CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) is the mean annual growth rate over a period. Formula: (Ending Value / Beginning Value)^(1/Years) − 1. Example: ₹1,00,000 growing to ₹2,00,000 in 5 years — (2)^(1/5) − 1 ≈ 14.87% per year.

How does CAGR interact with tax on my investment?

CAGR is measured over a fixed period — typically one financial year (ending 31 March in India) or the actual holding period of your investment.

What are India’s STCG and LTCG tax rates?

For equity mutual funds: STCG (held under 12 months) is taxed at 20%; LTCG (held over 12 months) is taxed at 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh per financial year. Debt funds are taxed at slab rates.

Is the new-regime 12% deeming relevant to CAGR?

The most common post-tax view is: compute CAGR on the gross value, then reduce the final value by the applicable capital-gains tax before comparing across instruments.

What is NIFTY 50’s long-run annual CAGR?

NIFTY 50 has historically delivered about an 11–12% CAGR over rolling 10-year periods. Actively managed equity funds may land in a 12–13% band over the same window, but past performance is not a promise.

How do I compare SIP returns using CAGR?

CAGR works only for a single lumpsum — for SIPs, use XIRR instead, which accounts for staggered cash flows. Both are covered by the InstaCalc toolkit.

What is PPF’s CAGR and how do I calculate it?

PPF’s rate is 7.1% for the current financial year. Over the full 15-year tenure the accrued CAGR roughly equals that rate because the PPF rate is government-fixed.

What is an FD’s CAGR and how do I compute it?

Since FDs are single-investment, CAGR = (Maturity/P)^(1/years) − 1. For a 7-year quarterly-compounded FD at 7%, CAGR comes to roughly 7.19%.

What is the CAGR of real estate in India?

Residential property in India has historically delivered 6–9% CAGR plus a 2–3% rental yield; returns vary by city, locality, and market cycle.

What is the difference between CAGR and XIRR?

CAGR applies to a single lumpsum with one start and one end value. XIRR handles multiple investments on different dates (SIP-style flows). Both tools exist in the InstaCalc toolkit.
