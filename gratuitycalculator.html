Understanding Gratuity Rules in India

Gratuity is a monetary benefit paid by the employer to an employee upon retirement or resignation after completing at least 5 years of services.


Act Covered Formula:
Gratuity = (15/26) × Last Drawn Salary × Years of Service
Note: Final tenure is rounded to the nearest year (e.g., 5 years 7 months = 6 years).


Not Covered Formula:
Gratuity = (15/30) × Average 10 Months Salary × Full Years of Service
Note: Rounding is not allowed; only full completed years are counted.


The maximum tax-exempt limit for gratuity in the private sector was increased to 20 Lakh by the government.

What is the 5-year eligibility rule?

To be eligible for gratuity, an employee typically must complete at least five continuous years of service with the same employer. Exception applies in case of death or disablement.

Is gratuity taxable?

For government employees, it is fully exempt. for private employees, it is exempt up to 20 Lakh (or the actual amount received/calculated, whichever is lower).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is gratuity?

Gratuity is a one-time lump sum an employer pays an employee after 5 years of continuous service, governed by the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.

What is the gratuity formula?

Gratuity = (Last drawn salary × 15 × years of service) ÷ 26, where last drawn salary includes basic plus dearness allowance.

Why is gratuity payable only after 5 years?

It is a condition of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 — an employee completes eligibility after 5 years of continuous service with the same employer.

Is gratuity taxable?

For private-sector employees covered by the Act, gratuity up to ₹20 lakh is tax-exempt; any amount above that is taxed as salary income.

What are the gratuity rules for Indian government employees?

Central and state government employees receive gratuity under separate service rules — 15 days' pay per completed year, with a ₹20 lakh ceiling.

Has the tax-free gratuity ceiling been raised recently?

Yes — the tax-free ceiling rose to ₹20 lakh following the 7th Pay Commission recommendation in 2024, matching the payment ceiling under the Act.

How quickly must the employer pay gratuity?

Employers must settle gratuity within 30 days of it becoming payable; delayed payment attracts simple interest under the Act.

Do I get gratuity if I switch jobs mid-career?

Eligibility resets with each employer — you need 5 continuous years with the same employer for that employer's gratuity to become payable.
