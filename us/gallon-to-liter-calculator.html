The Conversion Formulas & Constants

Fluid volume conversion factors depend on the legal standard applied:

  • 1 US Liquid Gallon = Exactly **3.785411784 Liters** ($231$ cubic inches, $128$ US fluid ounces).
  • 1 Imperial Gallon (UK) = Exactly **4.54609 Liters** ($160$ Imperial fluid ounces, approximately $20\%$ larger than a US gallon).
  • 1 US Dry Gallon = Exactly **$4.40488377$ Liters** (used primarily for agricultural grain and dry produce).
  • 1 Liter = Approximately **0.264172 US Liquid Gallons** ($1,000$ milliliters, $33.814$ US fluid ounces).

Fuel Economy Math: MPG to L/100km

Because miles per gallon is an efficiency metric (distance per volume) while liters per 100km is a consumption metric (volume per distance), they are inversely related:

L/100km = 235.215 ÷ MPG (US)   ⟺   MPG (US) = 235.215 ÷ (L/100km)

Worked Example: Cross-Border Gas Price Calculation

Suppose you are traveling to Canada or Europe and see gasoline priced at $1.65 CAD per liter, while US gas is priced at $3.45 USD per gallon (assuming 1 CAD = 0.73 USD):

  • Convert foreign price to USD: $1.65 \text{ CAD} \times 0.73 = \$1.2045 \text{ USD per liter}$.
  • Multiply by 3.78541: $1.2045 \times 3.78541 = $ **$4.56 USD per US Gallon**.
  • Verdict: The foreign fuel is equivalent to $4.56/gal, which is $1.11/gal more expensive than the US price.

Key Differences in Liquid Standards

  • Automotive Fuels: The US, Colombia, Belize, and Guatemala measure gasoline exclusively in US liquid gallons. Canada, Mexico, Europe, and Asia retail fuel in metric liters.
  • Fluid Ounces Discrepancy: A US fluid ounce is $29.57\text{ mL}$ ($128\text{ oz/gal}$), whereas an Imperial fluid ounce is $28.41\text{ mL}$ ($160\text{ oz/gal}$). Despite the smaller ounce, the Imperial gallon is larger overall.
  • Pints & Quarts: A US liquid pint is $16\text{ fl oz}$ ($473\text{ mL}$), while a British imperial pint is $20\text{ fl oz}$ ($568\text{ mL}$).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Confusing US & Imperial Gallons: Calculating vehicle fuel economy or pool chemical chlorine dosages using the wrong gallon constant causes a 20% dosage or mileage calculation error.
  • Linearizing MPG: Improving from 15 to 20 MPG saves 1.67 gallons per 100 miles, whereas improving from 45 to 50 MPG saves only 0.22 gallons per 100 miles. L/100km better illustrates fuel savings.
  • Dry vs Liquid Gallons: Using liquid gallon measurements for dry commodities like grain or bulk soil.

Frequently asked questions

How many liters in a US gallon?

Exactly 3.785411784 liters (1 US liquid gallon). The US and Imperial (UK) gallon differ — Imperial is 4.54609 liters.

What is a dry gallon?

A rarely used US dry-goods measure equal to about 4.405 liters — between the US liquid and Imperial gallon. Mostly historical.

How many liters in a UK gallon?

4.54609 liters — about 20 percent more than the US gallon. UK "mpg" figures look 20 percent better partly for this reason.

How do I convert mpg to L/100km?

Use 235.215 ÷ mpg (US gallons). A 25 mpg US car is about 9.4 L/100km. This calculator includes fuel-price conversion.

Why does the US still use gallons?

US Customary System predates metric and remains legally defined alongside metric under the 1988 Omnibus Trade Act.

How many gallons in a liter?

About 0.264 US gallons. Divide liters by 3.7854 to get gallons.

What is a US quart?

One-fourth of a US gallon — 0.946353 liters, very close to a liter. Milk, motor oil, and paint are commonly sold in quarts.

How do I convert fuel price per gallon to per liter?

Divide price per gallon by 3.7854. For example, $3.50 per gallon = about $0.925 per liter.

Is a beer barrel the same as an oil barrel?

No — a US beer barrel is 31 US gallons (117.3 L); an oil barrel is 42 US gallons (158.99 L). Confusing them causes ordering errors.

Why does "50 mpg UK" differ from "50 mpg US"?

UK imperial gallons are 20 percent larger, so a "50 mpg" UK figure means about 42 mpg US for the same fuel economy.
