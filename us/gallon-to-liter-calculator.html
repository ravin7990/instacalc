Gallon to Liter Conversion Formulas & Unit Standards

Standard conversion rates: 1 US Liquid Gallon = 3.785411784 Liters, 1 Liter = 0.264172 US Liquid Gallons, 1 UK Imperial Gallon = 4.54609 Liters, and 1 US Dry Gallon = 4.40488 Liters. In the United States, automotive fuel and beverages are measured in US liquid gallons, while scientific and global trade metrics use the metric liter (L).

Frequently Asked Questions

How many liters are in 1 US liquid gallon?

There are exactly 3.78541 liters in 1 US liquid gallon. To convert gallons to liters, multiply the gallon value by 3.78541.

What is the difference between a US gallon and an Imperial gallon?

A US liquid gallon is 3.78541 liters (128 US fl oz), whereas a UK Imperial gallon is 4.54609 liters (160 imperial fl oz), making the Imperial gallon approximately 20% larger.

How do you convert liters back to US gallons?

To convert liters to US liquid gallons, divide the number of liters by 3.78541 (or multiply by 0.264172).
