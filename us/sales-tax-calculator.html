State vs Local Rates & The 5 States with No Sales Tax (NOMAD)

In the United States, sales tax is levied at both the state level and local (county/city/special district) levels. Five US states have no statewide general sales tax (the NOMAD states: New Hampshire, Oregon, Montana, Alaska, and Delaware). In other states like California, Texas, Tennessee, and New York, combined state and municipal sales tax rates can range from 6% to over 10%.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which US states have no general sales tax?

Five states have no statewide sales tax (often remembered by the acronym NOMAD): New Hampshire, Oregon, Montana, Alaska, and Delaware.

How is combined US sales tax calculated on a receipt?

Combined sales tax is calculated by adding the state tax rate, county tax rate, and city/municipal tax rate together, then multiplying by the taxable purchase price. Formula: Tax = Price x (Combined Rate / 100).

Are groceries and prescription medicines subject to sales tax in the US?

In most US states, essential items like unprepared grocery food and prescription drugs are either completely exempt from sales tax or taxed at a reduced rate.
