US State & Local Sales Tax Calculator Guide

The InstaCalc US Sales Tax Calculator computes retail sales tax amounts, net prices, and total gross costs across all 50 United States and the District of Columbia. Unlike most nations that enforce a uniform national Value-Added Tax (VAT), the United States has no federal sales tax. Instead, retail sales tax is governed independently by individual states, counties, and municipal taxing jurisdictions. This utility supports both exclusive tax (adding tax to a sticker price) and inclusive/reverse tax (extracting net price and embedded sales tax from a receipt total).

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select Calculation Mode: Choose "Add Tax" (exclusive) to determine total price at checkout, or "Reverse Tax" (inclusive) to find the pre-tax base cost from a receipt.
  2. Enter Amount: Input your purchase or receipt dollar amount.
  3. Select Your State: Pick your state from the dropdown to load official base state tax rates.
  4. Add Local Surtax (Optional): Enter city, county, or special transit district tax percentages (typically 1.0% to 4.5%).
  5. View Detailed Breakdown: Instantly view base price, exact sales tax owed, and final gross total.

The Formulas: Exclusive vs Reverse Sales Tax

The sales tax calculation differs based on whether tax is added or extracted from the purchase:

1. Standard Exclusive Tax (Adding Tax at Checkout):

Sales Tax = Net Price × [(State Rate + Local Surtax) ÷ 100]

Total Price = Net Price + Sales Tax

2. Reverse Tax (Extracting Tax from Receipt Total):

Net Pre-Tax Price = Total Receipt Amount ÷ [1 + (Total Tax Rate ÷ 100)]

Embedded Tax Amount = Total Receipt Amount − Net Pre-Tax Price

Worked Example: Purchase in Los Angeles, California

Suppose you purchase a laptop with a sticker price of $1,200.00 in Los Angeles, CA (California state rate is 7.25% plus a local Los Angeles County surtax of 2.25%, totaling 9.50% combined sales tax):

  • Sticker Net Price: $1,200.00
  • Total Combined Tax Rate: 7.25% + 2.25% = 9.50%
  • Calculated Sales Tax: $1,200.00 × 0.095 = **$114.00**
  • Total Checkout Cost: $1,200.00 + $114.00 = **$1,314.00**

The Five "NOMAD" States with 0% State Sales Tax

Five US states do not levy any statewide general retail sales tax (often remembered by the acronym NOMAD):

  • New Hampshire (NH): 0% state sales tax (meals and lodging taxed separately).
  • Oregon (OR): 0% state sales tax (no local sales tax).
  • Montana (MT): 0% state sales tax (some resort municipalities levy local resort taxes).
  • Alaska (AK): 0% state sales tax (municipalities may levy local sales taxes up to 7.5%).
  • Delaware (DE): 0% state sales tax (imposes gross receipts tax on businesses instead).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Ignoring Local City Surtaxes: In states like Tennessee, Louisiana, and Washington, local county and city surtaxes can push combined rates above 9.5% or 10.0%.
  • Assuming All Goods Are Taxable: Most US states exempt unprepared grocery food, prescription drugs, and certain clothing items from sales tax.
  • Miscalculating Reverse Tax: Multiplying a $108 receipt by 8% gives $8.64, which is incorrect; the true pre-tax amount is $108 / 1.08 = $100 ($8.00 tax).

Frequently asked questions

Which states have no sales tax?

Five: Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire, and Oregon. Alaska allows local-option sales taxes in some boroughs.

What is the highest combined sales tax in the US?

Louisiana and Tennessee top the combined state plus average local rates above 9.5 percent. Some individual jurisdictions in California and Alabama exceed 10 percent.

Do online purchases have sales tax?

Yes — after the 2018 South Dakota v. Wayfair decision, most states collect sales tax from online sellers above an economic-nexus threshold, commonly 100,000 dollars in sales or 200 transactions.

Is shipping taxable?

It depends on the state. In some states (e.g. New York), separately stated shipping is not taxable; in others (e.g. Texas) it is.

Are groceries taxed?

Most states exempt groceries or apply a reduced rate, but a handful (Alabama, Mississippi, South Dakota, others) apply the full sales tax to food.

What is "use tax"?

The companion tax owed when you buy in a no-sales-tax state (or online without paying tax) and use the item in your home state. It is filed on your state income tax return and rarely enforced for small purchases.

How do I reverse-calculate tax from a total?

Divide the tax-included total by 1 plus the rate. For example, 108.25 dollars at 8.25 percent = 108.25 ÷ 1.0825 = 100 dollars pre-tax, with 8.25 dollars tax.

Are services taxed?

Varies — professional services (legal, medical, accounting) are generally not taxed, but some states (New Mexico, Hawaii, South Dakota) tax most services. Repair services are taxed in several states.

Do I pay both state and local rates?

Yes — the combined rate is state + county + city + special district. A "4 percent state tax" state can charge 9.5 percent combined in a high-rate city.

What changes when I cross state lines to buy?

The seller charges the rate where the item is delivered, not where you live. Purchases in no-tax states still owe your state's use tax on bring-home items.
