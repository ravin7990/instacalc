InstaCalc USA — Privacy & Google Play Compliance

Users who have signed into any of our mobile applications or web utilities via Google Sign-In or direct registration may request the permanent deletion of their account profile and all associated data at any time under Google Play Developer policies and the CCPA.

What Data We Remove

  • Google authentication identifiers and registered email addresses
  • Locally saved calculation presets or history
  • Personal app preferences and customization logs

Processing Timeline

Deletion requests are prioritized and completed within 7 business days. Upon completion, all identifiers are permanently purged from production systems.
