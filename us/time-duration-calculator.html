The Mathematics of Sexagesimal Time Math

Time calculation uses the base-60 (sexagesimal) numbering system. When adding minutes or seconds that exceed 59, the sum carries over into the next higher unit:

Total Seconds = (S₁ + S₂) mod 60,   Carry to Minutes = ⌊(S₁ + S₂) ÷ 60⌋

Total Minutes = (M₁ + M₂ + Carry) mod 60,   Carry to Hours = ⌊(M₁ + M₂ + Carry) ÷ 60⌋

Converting to Decimal Hours for Billing:

Decimal Hours = Hours + (Minutes ÷ 60) + (Seconds ÷ 3,600)

Worked Example: Aviation Pilot Flight Log

Suppose a commercial pilot logs three consecutive flight legs in a single duty day:

  • Leg 1: 2 hours, 45 minutes, 20 seconds
  • Leg 2: 3 hours, 28 minutes, 50 seconds
  • Leg 3: 1 hour, 55 minutes, 15 seconds

Step-by-Step Summation:

  • Seconds: $20 + 50 + 15 = 85 \text{ seconds} = 1\text{ min } 25\text{ sec}$ (Carry 1 minute).
  • Minutes: $45 + 28 + 55 + 1 = 129 \text{ minutes} = 2\text{ hrs } 09\text{ min}$ (Carry 2 hours).
  • Hours: $2 + 3 + 1 + 2 = $ **8 hours**.
  • Final Total Duration: **8 hours, 9 minutes, 25 seconds**.
  • Decimal Equivalent: $8 + (9/60) + (25/3600) = $ **$8.1569$ decimal hours**.

Practical Use Cases

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Decimal Confusion: Treating 2 hours and 45 minutes as 2.45 hours (it is 2.75 hours). 45 minutes divided by 60 equals 0.75.
  • Forgetting 24-Hour Midnight Crossovers: Subtracting a 10:00 PM start from a 2:00 AM finish without adding 24 hours yields negative time.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add or subtract hours and minutes?

Add directly (45 minutes + 30 minutes = 75 minutes = 1 hour 15 minutes). The calculator auto-normalizes the result.

What is the difference between clock time and duration?

Clock time is a point (3 PM); duration is a span (2 hours 15 minutes). Duration arithmetic doesn't carry AM/PM or day boundaries.

How do I calculate time across midnight?

Add 24 hours to the end time before subtracting. 10 PM to 2 AM is (2+24) − 22 = 4 hours.

What is 90 minutes in decimal?

1.5 hours. Payroll systems commonly use 1.5 rather than 1:30 for overtime calculations.

How many seconds in an hour?

3,600. In a day: 86,400. Useful when converting runtimes or batch durations to totals.

How do I sum multiple durations?

Convert each to the same unit (minutes or seconds), sum, then re-convert. Standard practice in video editing and payroll.

Does Daylight Saving Time affect duration?

Pure duration arithmetic ignores DST — a 3-hour span is still 3 hours. Clock-time transitions across a DST boundary add or lose an hour.

What is H:MM:SS?

Standard notation for hours, minutes, seconds. 1:30:45 means one hour, thirty minutes, forty-five seconds.

How do I subtract a date and time together?

Convert to a single unit (seconds since epoch or day-fraction) and subtract. This calculator supports date+time inputs.

How does video runtime work?

Video durations use frame counts at 24/25/30/60 fps — often 23.976 fps in consumer submissions. Convert frames to seconds before summing with clock-time durations.
