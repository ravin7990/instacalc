How to Add and Subtract Elapsed Time Durations

Enter hours, minutes, and seconds for multiple time intervals to add them together or find the difference between two elapsed durations. You can also multiply a duration by a quantity (e.g., video production times, athletic lap splits, or audio tracks) and convert the sum into total seconds, total minutes, or total decimal hours.

Sexagesimal (Base-60) Arithmetic & Decimal Conversion Guide

Time arithmetic operates in base-60 (sexagesimal), where 60 seconds equal 1 minute and 60 minutes equal 1 hour. Converting sexagesimal time to decimal fractions requires dividing minutes by 60 and seconds by 3600 (e.g., 2 hours, 36 minutes, 18 seconds = 2 + 36/60 + 18/3600 = 2.605 decimal hours).

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add two time durations together?

Add seconds first (carry over each 60 seconds as 1 minute), add minutes (carry over each 60 minutes as 1 hour), and finally add the hours together.

How do you calculate running pace per mile?

Convert total elapsed running time into total minutes, then divide by the total miles ran. For example, 45 minutes for 5 miles equals a pace of 9 minutes per mile.

How do you convert seconds to hours, minutes, and seconds format?

Divide total seconds by 3600 to get hours, take the remaining seconds and divide by 60 to get minutes, with the remainder being leftover seconds.
