How to Calculate Days Between Dates & Workdays

Choose the Start Date and End Date. The calculator calculates total calendar days, elapsed weeks, remaining days, and total working business days (Monday through Friday). You can toggle the inclusion/exclusion of the start date, weekends, and US Federal Holidays (such as Memorial Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Juneteenth).

US Federal Holidays & Business Day Calculations

The US federal calendar recognizes 11 official federal holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Washington's Birthday (Presidents' Day), Memorial Day, Juneteenth National Independence Day, Independence Day (4th of July), Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. When a federal holiday falls on a weekend, it is typically observed on the preceding Friday or following Monday.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many business days are in a typical US year?

A standard calendar year contains 260 to 262 weekdays. After subtracting the 11 US Federal Holidays, there are approximately 249 to 251 official working days.

What happens when a US federal holiday falls on a weekend?

If a federal holiday falls on a Sunday, the following Monday is observed as a paid holiday. If it falls on a Saturday, the preceding Friday is observed.

Can this calculator add or subtract days from a given date?

Yes, switch to the 'Add / Subtract Days' mode to compute a future or past deadline by specifying an integer number of calendar or business days.
