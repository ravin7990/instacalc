Calculate Days Between Dates & US Working Business Days

The InstaCalc Days & Business Days Calculator computes the exact calendar duration between two dates and determines the number of working business days by excluding weekends and all 11 official United States Federal Holidays. Whether you are managing legal court filing deadlines, commercial lease closing schedules, payroll processing periods, or supply chain shipping intervals, this utility provides reliable, regulation-compliant day counts with calendar date arithmetic.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select Start Date: Enter the beginning date (MM/DD/YYYY).
  2. Select End Date: Enter the target or ending date.
  3. Configure Inclusions: Choose whether to include or exclude the start date in the final tally.
  4. Toggle US Federal Holidays: Check the "Exclude US Federal Holidays" option to filter out legally recognized government and banking non-working days.
  5. View Output Breakdown: Instantly see total calendar days, elapsed business days, total weekend days, and full week intervals.

Calculation Logic & Federal Holiday Rules

Calendar days represent the exact integer difference between two dates:

Calendar Days = Target Date − Start Date

Business days iterate across every calendar day in the range and increment only if the day satisfies two conditions:

  1. The day of the week is Monday through Friday (Days 1–5).
  2. The date does not coincide with an official United States Federal Holiday.

The 11 Official US Federal Holidays Recognized:

  • New Year's Day: January 1
  • Martin Luther King Jr. Day: Third Monday in January
  • Washington's Birthday (Presidents' Day): Third Monday in February
  • Memorial Day: Last Monday in May
  • Juneteenth National Independence Day: June 19
  • Independence Day: July 4
  • Labor Day: First Monday in September
  • Columbus Day (Indigenous Peoples' Day): Second Monday in October
  • Veterans Day: November 11
  • Thanksgiving Day: Fourth Thursday in November
  • Christmas Day: December 25

Federal "Shift Rule": When a fixed federal holiday falls on a Saturday, federal employees observe it on the preceding Friday. If it falls on a Sunday, it is legally observed on the following Monday.

Worked Example: Real Estate Escrow Timeline

Suppose a commercial contract executes on November 10, 2026, specifying a 30-calendar-day inspection contingency ending on December 10, 2026:

  • Total Elapsed Calendar Days: 30 calendar days (4 weeks and 2 days).
  • Total Weekend Days: 8 weekend days (4 Saturdays, 4 Sundays).
  • Federal Holidays Occurring: 2 days (Veterans Day on Nov 11, Thanksgiving on Nov 26).
  • Net Working Business Days: **20 business days**.

Legal & Financial Applications

  • Federal Court Rule 6(a): In US legal proceedings, when computing statutory response deadlines under 11 days, intermediate weekends and legal holidays are typically excluded.
  • Banking & ACH Settlement: Federal Reserve FedACH clearing operates on banking days; transfers initiated on Friday evening do not settle until Tuesday due to weekend non-clearing.
  • SEC 10-K & 10-Q Filing Windows: Corporate disclosure filings use exact business day counting from fiscal quarter ends.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Ignoring the Federal "Observed" Monday Rule: Believing a July 4 holiday falling on Sunday means Monday is a normal banking day.
  • Counting the Starting Day Twice: Always establish whether day zero counts as day one in your governing contract.
  • Assuming State Holidays are Federal: Holidays like Patriot's Day (MA) or Cesar Chavez Day (CA) are state-specific and do not close federal courts or national banks.

Frequently asked questions

How many business days are in a year?

About 260 weekdays, minus 11 federal holidays = roughly 249 business days, though this varies with weekend placement around holidays.

What are the 11 US federal holidays?

New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas.

What happens when a federal holiday falls on a weekend?

Federal holidays falling on Saturday are observed the prior Friday; those on Sunday are observed Monday.

How are leap years handled?

Every 4 years has 366 days, except century years not divisible by 400 (1700, 1800, 1900 were common years; 2000 was a leap year).

Are calendar days the same as business days?

No — a "calendar day" includes weekends and holidays; a "business day" excludes Saturdays, Sundays, and federal holidays. Contracts must specify which.

How do I add X days to a date?

Add directly to the day-of-month, with automatic month/year rollovers. This calculator handles the arithmetic and leap-day transitions.

What date-count convention does finance use?

30/360 for some bonds, Actual/Actual for US Treasuries, and Actual/365-fixed for most adjustable-rate loans — the convention names the math.

How do I count excluding the end date?

Default behavior excludes the end date — result is the strict difference. Include it by adding one to the count (common for hotel-night or exam-window counting).

Does DST affect day counting?

No — DST shifts hours within a day, not the day count. Only cross-timezone hour calculations need DST handling.

What is the fairest way to deliver a "30-day notice"?

Specify calendar days (most common default), a specific date ("by March 15"), or business days for corporate contracts. Ambiguity triggers disputes.
