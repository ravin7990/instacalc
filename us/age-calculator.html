How to Calculate Your Exact Age & Time Passed

Select your exact date of birth (and optional birth time) and choose the target date of calculation (defaults to today). The calculator computes your exact chronological age in completed calendar years, remaining months, and remaining days. It also breaks down your lifespan into total days, total hours, total minutes, and total seconds lived, factoring in every leap year precisely.

US Age Milestones & Important Legal Horizons

Key American age milestones include age 18 (voting, legal adulthood), age 21 (legal drinking, car rental independence), age 26 (transition off parental health insurance under the ACA), age 59½ (penalty-free retirement account distributions from 401k/IRAs), age 62 (earliest Social Security claiming age), age 65 (Medicare eligibility), and age 67 (Full Retirement Age for Social Security for those born 1960 and later).

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the age calculator handle leap years?

The calculator automatically accounts for leap years (containing February 29th) during elapsed interval calculations, ensuring exact day counts between any two historical or future dates.

What are the key US retirement age milestones?

Major US age milestones include age 59½ for penalty-free IRA/401(k) withdrawals, age 62 for early Social Security, age 65 for Medicare enrollment, and age 67 for full Social Security benefits.

How is half-birthday calculated?

A half-birthday is exactly six calendar months from your birth date, adjusted for months with differing day lengths (28, 30, or 31 days).
