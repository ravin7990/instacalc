How to Calculate Your FIRE Number & Retirement Horizon

Enter your current annual living expenses, current investment portfolio value, annual savings amount, expected investment growth rate, and desired Safe Withdrawal Rate (SWR, typically 3.5% to 4.0%). The calculator computes your core FIRE target number, estimated years to reach financial independence, and milestones across Lean FIRE (minimalist living), Regular FIRE (maintaining current lifestyle), and Fat FIRE (abundant retirement lifestyle).

The 4% Safe Withdrawal Rule & Trinity Study Principles

The 4% Rule, established by the Trinity Study, suggests that withdrawing 4% of your initial portfolio value in year one of retirement (adjusted for inflation each subsequent year) provides a 95%+ probability of your money lasting 30 years in a balanced stock/bond allocation. Your FIRE number is simply: FIRE Number = Annual Expenses x 25 (at 4% SWR), or Annual Expenses x 28.5 (at a conservative 3.5% SWR).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the FIRE Number formula?

Your FIRE Number is calculated as Annual Retirement Expenses divided by your Safe Withdrawal Rate (SWR). Using the traditional 4% rule, your FIRE number is Annual Expenses x 25.

What is the difference between Lean FIRE and Fat FIRE?

Lean FIRE targets minimalist living with annual expenses typically under $40,000/yr, while Fat FIRE provides for an affluent lifestyle with expenses exceeding $100,000+/yr.

What is Coast FIRE?

Coast FIRE is the milestone where your existing investment balance is large enough that it will compound to your full retirement target by traditional retirement age without needing any additional contributions.
