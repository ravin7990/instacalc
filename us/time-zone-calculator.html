The Four Continental US Time Zones & Offsets

The contiguous United States spans four primary standard time zones, each separated by exactly one hour:

  • Eastern Time (ET): UTC-5 during Standard Time (EST) / UTC-4 during Daylight Saving Time (EDT). Dominates New York, Washington D.C., Atlanta, and Miami.
  • Central Time (CT): UTC-6 (CST) / UTC-5 (CDT). Exactly **1 hour behind Eastern**. Dominates Chicago, Dallas, Houston, and Minneapolis.
  • Mountain Time (MT): UTC-7 (MST) / UTC-6 (MDT). Exactly **2 hours behind Eastern**. Dominates Denver, Salt Lake City, and Albuquerque.
  • Pacific Time (PT): UTC-8 (PST) / UTC-7 (PDT). Exactly **3 hours behind Eastern**. Dominates Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle, and Las Vegas.

Alaska & Hawaii Non-Contiguous Zones:

  • Alaska Time (AKT): UTC-9 (AKST) / UTC-8 (AKDT). 4 hours behind Eastern Time.
  • Hawaii-Aleutian Time (HAT): UTC-10 (HST). 5 hours behind Eastern (or 6 hours behind EDT during summer).

Worked Example: Nationwide Team All-Hands Call

Suppose a nationwide company schedules a virtual all-hands meeting for 2:00 PM Eastern Time on a Wednesday:

  • Eastern Time (New York / Miami): 2:00 PM
  • Central Time (Chicago / Austin): 1:00 PM (1 hour earlier)
  • Mountain Time (Denver / Salt Lake): 12:00 PM (2 hours earlier)
  • Pacific Time (Seattle / San Francisco): 11:00 AM (3 hours earlier)
  • Alaska Time (Anchorage): 10:00 AM
  • Hawaii Time (Honolulu): 8:00 AM or 9:00 AM (depending on DST)

Daylight Saving Time & The Arizona Exception

Under the Uniform Time Act of 1966, Daylight Saving Time begins on the second Sunday in March (clocks "spring forward" one hour) and ends on the first Sunday in November (clocks "fall back" one hour). However:

  • Arizona: Most of Arizona does not observe Daylight Saving Time and stays on Mountain Standard Time (MST, UTC-7) all year. During the summer, Arizona has the same clock time as Pacific Daylight Time (PDT).
  • Navajo Nation: The Navajo reservation in northeastern Arizona does observe Daylight Saving Time, creating a local time zone island within the state.
  • Hawaii: Does not observe Daylight Saving Time, maintaining UTC-10 year-round.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Forgetting Arizona's Changing Offset: Assuming Denver and Phoenix always share the same clock time; during the summer, Denver is on MDT while Phoenix is on MST (1 hour difference).
  • Cross-Midnight Scheduling: A 9:00 PM Pacific flight lands on the East Coast the next calendar morning due to the 3-hour forward jump plus flight time.

Understanding the 3-Hour Coast-to-Coast Time Spread

The continental United States spans three hours: Eastern Time is one hour ahead of Central, two hours ahead of Mountain, and three hours ahead of Pacific Time. A 9:00 AM call in San Francisco (PT) corresponds to 12:00 PM Noon in New York City (ET).

US States and Territories That Do Not Observe Daylight Saving Time

While most of the nation shifts clocks forward in March and back in November, Hawaii and most of Arizona (excluding the Navajo Nation) remain on Standard Time year-round. US territories (Puerto Rico, Guam, US Virgin Islands, and American Samoa) also do not observe DST.

Converting Coordinated Universal Time (UTC/GMT) to US Time Zones

Software servers and aviation logs operate on Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). Standard time offsets are UTC-5 for EST, UTC-6 for CST, UTC-7 for MST, and UTC-8 for PST (advancing by one hour during Daylight Saving Time to EDT UTC-4, CDT UTC-5, MDT UTC-6, and PDT UTC-7).

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the four primary continental US time zones and their UTC offsets?

Eastern Time (UTC-5 EST / UTC-4 EDT), Central Time (UTC-6 CST / UTC-5 CDT), Mountain Time (UTC-7 MST / UTC-6 MDT), and Pacific Time (UTC-8 PST / UTC-7 PDT). Alaska (UTC-9/8) and Hawaii (UTC-10) form the fifth and sixth zones.

Which US states and territories do not observe Daylight Saving Time?

Hawaii and most of Arizona (excluding the Navajo Nation) do not observe Daylight Saving Time and remain on standard time year-round. US territories (Puerto Rico, Guam, US Virgin Islands, American Samoa) also do not observe DST.

How do you convert meeting times between Pacific (PST/PDT) and Eastern (EST/EDT)?

Eastern Time is exactly 3 hours ahead of Pacific Time. A 9:00 AM meeting in California (PT) takes place at 12:00 PM Noon in New York (ET). A 2:00 PM meeting in ET is 11:00 AM in PT.

What is the difference between standard time (EST) and daylight time (EDT)?

Standard time (e.g. EST) applies during winter months (November to March). Daylight time (e.g. EDT) advances clocks by one hour during spring and summer (March to November) to extend evening daylight.
