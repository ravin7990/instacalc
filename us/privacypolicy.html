Client-Side Architecture

1. Zero Server-Side Calculation Logging

Every calculator on InstaCalc USA (including our 401(k), US Mortgage, Paycheck, Roth IRA, and FIRE tools) executes its math entirely inside your local web browser using JavaScript. The numbers you enter—such as your annual salary, mortgage borrowing amounts, or credit card balances—are never uploaded, logged, or stored on our servers.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Social Security Numbers (SSN), taxpayer identification, or banking credentials.
  • Personal financial profiles, credit scores, or account balances.
  • Biometric or sensitive geolocation data.
California & State Consumer Privacy

3. CCPA / CPRA & VCDPA Compliance

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (VCDPA), residents have specific rights regarding personal information:

  • Right to Know: You may request disclosure of categories of data collected.
  • Right to Delete: You may request deletion of any collected technical telemetry.
  • Do Not Sell or Share: We do not sell or share your personal information to third-party data brokers.
  • Non-Discrimination: You will never receive degraded service or pricing for exercising privacy rights.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Cookies

We use Google Analytics and Firebase to analyze website traffic volume, detect broken links, and measure Core Web Vitals. These services capture anonymized metrics such as browser type, referring domain, and operating system. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using Google's official browser add-on.

5. Advertising Partners (Google AdSense)

To keep our calculation tools 100% free, we partner with Google AdSense to display non-personalized and personalized advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage or disable ad personalization by visiting Google Ad Settings.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

InstaCalc USA is intended for general audience use by individuals aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Frequently Asked Questions About Privacy

Are my salary, mortgage, or financial calculations saved on your servers?

No. All calculation models execute locally within your web browser (client-side). None of your inputs, salaries, loan amounts, or savings figures are transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Does InstaCalc USA comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)?

Yes. We respect all consumer rights under the CCPA and CPRA. We do not sell or share personal information with third-party data brokers.

What analytics cookies are used on the site?

We use standard Google Analytics to gather aggregated, anonymized metrics such as browser type, page visit counts, and device categories to optimize performance.

Can I request deletion of my data?

Because all calculator interactions run locally and require no registration, we hold no user profiles or financial histories. For support inquiries or technical telemetry deletion, submit our Data Removal form.

How does InstaCalc make money if the tools are free?

Our free calculators are supported through non-intrusive contextual display advertising via Google AdSense. We do not sell user data to monetize our platforms.
