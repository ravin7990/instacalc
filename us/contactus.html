Helpful Tips for Fast Support

When reporting calculation discrepancies, please include:

  • The specific tool URL (e.g. /us/mortgage-calculator.html)
  • Your exact input parameters
  • Expected result vs InstaCalc output
  • Source citation (IRS, Fannie Mae, etc.)

Frequently Asked Questions About Support

How quickly does the InstaCalc support team respond?

We typically review and reply to all email inquiries and feature requests within 24 to 48 hours during business days.

How can I report a calculation discrepancy or bug?

Select 'Bug Report / Math Discrepancy' in the contact form, specify the tool URL, and describe the input values and expected outcome.

Can I request a custom calculator for my organization?

Yes. We frequently develop and publish new public financial and utility calculators based on user demand.

Can I embed InstaCalc tools on my website?

Currently, we do not support direct iframe embedding, but you are welcome to link directly to any tool on our site.

Is there phone support available?

To keep all calculators 100% free, we provide support exclusively via email and our online contact form.

How is my contact information handled?

Your name and email address are used solely to reply to your inquiry. We never sell or share contact details with advertisers.

Do you accept guest articles or sponsored posts?

No. To maintain editorial integrity and objectivity, all guide content is written and maintained exclusively by our internal team.

Can I request feature additions like new currency formats or metric units?

Yes! We welcome suggestions for new unit conversions, slider ranges, or preset scenario buttons.

How do I request removal of submitted data?

If you submitted a message and wish to have your contact record deleted, email developerravin@gmail.com with 'Data Removal' in the subject line.

Who handles business partnership and licensing inquiries?

All partnership, API integration, and business licensing inquiries are reviewed directly by our founder, Ravin Pednekar.
