How to Calculate Concrete for Slabs, Footings & Columns

Select your project shape (rectangular slab, round column/pier, or circular footing). Enter dimensions in feet or inches (Length, Width, and Thickness/Depth). The calculator outputs the exact volume in cubic feet (ft³) and cubic yards (yd³). It also calculates the exact number of pre-mixed 80-lb and 60-lb bags required, and estimated cost for ready-mix truck delivery.

Cubic Yards Formula & Pre-Mix Bag Estimates

To calculate cubic yards manually for a rectangular slab: Cubic Yards = (Length in feet x Width in feet x Thickness in feet) / 27. For standard pre-mixed concrete bags: one 80-lb bag yields approximately 0.60 cu ft (45 bags per cu yd), and one 60-lb bag yields approximately 0.45 cu ft (60 bags per cu yd). Always add a 10% waste buffer to account for uneven grade, spillage, and slab settling.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many 80 lb bags of concrete make 1 cubic yard?

It takes 45 bags of 80 lb pre-mixed concrete (or 60 bags of 60 lb concrete) to yield 1 cubic yard (27 cubic feet) of poured concrete.

What is the formula to calculate concrete volume in cubic yards?

Multiply Length (ft) x Width (ft) x Depth (ft) to get cubic feet, then divide by 27. Formula: Cubic Yards = (Length x Width x Depth in inches / 12) / 27.

Why should I add 10% extra concrete to my order?

Adding a 5% to 10% buffer accounts for sub-base grade variations, formwork deflection, spillage, and slab depth inconsistencies, preventing costly shortages mid-pour.
