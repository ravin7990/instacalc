Pythagorean Theorem (d = √(w² + h²)) & 3D Box Diagonals

For any 2D rectangle with width w and height h, the diagonal length is d = √(w² + h²). For a 3D box, room, or shipping parcel with width w, length l, and height h, the 3D space diagonal from corner to opposite corner is d = √(w² + l² + h²). In carpentry and construction, builders use the 3-4-5 Pythagorean triple (3² + 4² = 5²) to verify that corners and foundation forms are perfectly square (90 degrees).

Frequently Asked Questions

How is TV screen size measured?

TV and computer monitor sizes are always measured diagonally from the top-left viewable corner to the bottom-right viewable corner, excluding the outer bezel.

What is the 3-4-5 rule for checking square corners in carpentry?

Measure 3 feet along one wall, 4 feet along the adjacent wall, and verify the diagonal distance is exactly 5 feet. If the diagonal is exactly 5 feet, the corner is a true 90-degree right angle.

What is the formula for a 3D diagonal in a room or shipping box?

The 3D space diagonal formula is d = √(Length² + Width² + Height²), which gives the straight-line distance across opposite corners in three dimensions.
