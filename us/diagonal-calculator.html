The Pythagorean Formula Behind the Math

The diagonal of any right-angled rectangle is the Euclidean hypotenuse linking opposite corners:

d = √(w² + h²)

Where:

  • d: Diagonal distance connecting opposite corners.
  • w: Width or base of the rectangle.
  • h: Height or length of the rectangle.

Worked Example: TV Screen Dimensions

Televisions are marketed commercially by their diagonal screen measurement across a standard 16:9 widescreen aspect ratio. For a 65-inch TV screen:

  • Aspect Ratio Equation: $\text{Width} = 16x, \quad \text{Height} = 9x$.
  • Diagonal Formulation: $\sqrt{(16x)^2 + (9x)^2} = \sqrt{337} x \approx 18.3576 x = 65\text{ inches}$.
  • Solving for $x$: $x \approx 3.5407$.
  • Actual Width: $16 \times 3.5407 = $ **$56.65$ inches**.
  • Actual Height: $9 \times 3.5407 = $ **$31.87$ inches**.
  • Total Screen Viewing Area: $56.65 \times 31.87 = $ **$1,805.4$ sq inches** ($12.54\text{ sq ft}$).

Construction Squaring: The 3-4-5 Rule

Professional carpenters, deck builders, and masons verify that corners are true 90-degree right angles using Pythagorean triples:

  • Measure 3 feet along one wall or form board from the corner and mark it.
  • Measure 4 feet along the perpendicular board from the same corner and mark it.
  • Measure the diagonal span between the two marks. If the corner is square, the diagonal will measure exactly 5 feet ($3^2 + 4^2 = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5^2$).
  • For larger structures such as house footings, scale up the triple to 6-8-10 or 9-12-15 feet for greater precision.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Measuring Outside Beveled Framing: Always measure from inside string lines or the true corner apex; measuring from flared footing edges introduces angular skew.
  • TV Bezel Distortion: Believing a 65" TV fits in a 65" wide cabinet. The 65" is diagonal; the width is ~56.7", but TV frame bezels and stand legs add 1–2 inches.
  • Mixing Fractional and Decimal Units: Ensure fractions of an inch (such as $3/8" = 0.375"$) are properly converted before calculation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the rectangle diagonal formula?

d = √(length² + width²), the Pythagorean theorem. A 16×9 cm rectangle has a diagonal of √337 ≈ 18.36 cm.

What is a square's diagonal?

d = s × √2. A 10 cm square has a diagonal of about 14.14 cm.

What is the 3-4-5 rule in carpentry?

If legs are in a 3:4 ratio, the diagonal is exactly 5 units — a fast integer right-angle check used on decks, frames, and foundations.

How do I check if a room is square?

Measure both diagonals corner-to-corner. If they match within 0.5 percent (about 3-4 mm per meter), the room is square.

How is TV screen size measured?

As the diagonal in inches, excluding the bezel. A "55-inch" TV has a 55-inch panel diagonal — roughly 47.9 × 26.96 inches at 16:9 aspect.

What is the space diagonal of a 3D box?

The inside corner-to-opposite-corner line, d = √(l² + w² + h²). Useful for shipping — the longest item to fit inside a box.

How many diagonals does a polygon have?

Formula: n(n−3) ÷ 2. A pentagon has 5; a hexagon 9; a decagon 35.

Are a rhombus's diagonals perpendicular?

Always — they bisect each other at right angles. Given one diagonal p and side a, the other is q = 2 × √(a² − (p/2)²).

How do I find a parallelogram diagonal?

Using the law of cosines: d = √(a² + b² − 2ab·cos θ), where a and b are adjacent sides and θ the included angle.

What is the diagonal of a cube?

d = s × √3 — the space-diagonal formula when all three dimensions are equal.
