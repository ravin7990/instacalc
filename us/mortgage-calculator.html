Mortgage Calculator helps you see the real monthly cost of a US home purchase — not just principal and interest, but taxes, insurance, PMI, and HOA fees on top.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the home purchase price.
  2. Enter your down payment as a percent (20% avoids PMI on conventional loans).
  3. Set the loan term (30-year is standard, 15-year saves interest).
  4. Enter the interest rate — check current Freddie Mac PMMS averages for a realistic figure.
  5. Add annual property tax, annual homeowners insurance, monthly HOA fees, and estimated PMI rate if down < 20%.
  6. Click Calculate — the monthly breakdown spans principal, interest, taxes, insurance, PMI, and HOA.

The method behind the math

Monthly P&I uses the standard amortization formula: P&I = P × r / (1 − (1+r)^−n), where P is the loan amount (price minus down payment), r the monthly rate (APR÷12), and n the number of months. Then taxes ÷ 12, insurance ÷ 12, PMI (loan × rate ÷ 12), and HOA are added on top. This matches how US lenders quote "PITI + HOA" in a Loan Estimate.

Worked example

On a $450,000 home with $90,000 down (20%), a 30-year fixed at 6.5%, $4,500/yr property tax, $1,800/yr insurance, and no HOA: loan is $360,000 at 0.5417% monthly over 360 months, giving P&I of about $2,275/mo. Adding property tax $375/mo and insurance $150/mo produces a total monthly cost of about $2,800 — a figure many first-time buyers underestimate by 30-40%.

Rules and regulations that affect your result

  • Conventional loans require PMI below 20% down, typically 0.3-1.5% of the loan per year, removable once you reach 20% equity.
  • FHA loans (3.5% down minimum) require both upfront (1.75%) and annual mortgage insurance premiumsPaid for the life of the loan if you put under 10% down.
  • VA loans (eligible veterans and service members) have no monthly PMI but a one-time funding fee of 1.25-3.3%.
  • Property tax rates vary widely by state — New Jersey averages ~2.2% of home value, Hawaii ~0.3%.
  • The 2025 conforming loan limit is $806,500 in most counties, beyond which you need a jumbo loan.

Practical tips

  • Round up: paying $100 extra per month on a 30-year loan can save ~$35,000 in interest and shave ~3 years.
  • A 15-year fixed typically runs 0.5-0.75 percentage points lower than a 30-year and cuts total interest by more than half.
  • Points ("discount points") each cost 1% of the loan and reduce the rate by roughly 0.25% — worth it if you stay past the break-even period (~5-7 years).
  • Avoid adjustable-rate mortgages if you plan to stay in the home past the fixed period — rate reset risk is real.
  • Get pre-approved before house-hunting; sellers in competitive markets expect it.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Comparing lenders by rate alone without comparing total Loan Estimate closing costs.
  • Forgetting to budget for maintenance (1-2% of home value per year is the standard recommendation).
  • Assuming the listing-price mortgage is the only cost — HOA, utilities, and repairs add 15-25% to true monthly cost.
  • Emptying savings for a 20% down payment and skipping the emergency fund, then missing payments when a repair hits.
  • Not shopping the rate — Fannie Mae research shows an average buyer saves ~$1,500 by getting just one additional quote.

Frequently asked questions

How much house can I afford?

The 28/36 rule: housing costs should stay under 28 percent of gross monthly income, and total debt under 36 percent. On an 85,000 dollar salary that suggests a mortgage payment near 1,980 dollars, including taxes and insurance.

What is PMI and when can I remove it?

Private Mortgage Insurance is 0.3 to 1.5 percent of the loan per year, required on conventional loans below 20 percent down. It is removable once you reach 20 percent equity through payments or an appraisal.

Should I choose a 15-year or 30-year mortgage?

The 30-year has lower monthly payments but much more total interest; the 15-year usually runs 0.5 to 0.75 percentage points lower and cuts total interest by more than half. Choose 30-year if cash flow is tight, 15-year to save.

What are closing costs on a US mortgage?

Typically 2 to 5 percent of the loan amount — appraisal, title, origination, points, escrow, and recording fees. Always compare the Loan Estimates from at least three lenders.

What is the current conforming loan limit for 2025?

806,500 dollars in most US counties, higher in high-cost areas. Loans above this are "jumbo" mortgages with stricter requirements.

How does an ARM (adjustable-rate mortgage) work?

The rate is fixed for an initial period (5, 7, or 10 years), then adjusts annually based on an index plus a margin. Rate caps limit yearly adjustment, but resets can still jump payments significantly.

Is it better to put 20 percent down or invest the extra cash?

Twenty percent down avoids PMI, cutting monthly cost. But if the mortgage rate is 5 percent and your investment return is 7 percent, investing extra may outperform — factoring in liquidity and risk preference.

How much does property tax add to a mortgage payment?

Typically 1 to 2 percent of home value per year, escrowed in monthly installments. In New Jersey the average is over 2 percent, in Hawaii under 0.4 percent.

What credit score do I need?

Conventional loans require a minimum score of 620; FHA loans accept 580 with 3.5 percent down (500 with 10 percent). Better credit means a lower rate — a 740-versus-680 score difference is roughly 0.5 to 1 percentage point.

Can I pay off my mortgage early?

Most conforming loans have no prepayment penalty (verify in your note). Extra principal payments slash total interest — an extra 100 dollars per month on a 30-year 360,000 dollar loan saves roughly 35,000 dollars.
