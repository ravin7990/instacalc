How to Estimate Your Monthly US Mortgage Payment (PITI)

Enter your home purchase price, down payment (in dollars or percentage), loan term (30-year or 15-year fixed), interest rate (APR), annual property tax rate (typically 0.8% to 2.2% depending on state/county), annual homeowners insurance premium, and estimated PMI (private mortgage insurance for down payments under 20%). The calculator computes your total monthly PITI payment and generates an amortization schedule.

Principal, Interest, Property Taxes, Insurance & PMI Breakdown

A complete monthly mortgage payment (PITI) includes Principal (repaying the borrowed loan), Interest (the lender's fee), Property Taxes (collected into escrow for local county/city services), and Homeowners Insurance (hazard protection). If your down payment is less than 20% on a conventional loan, Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) is required until your loan-to-value (LTV) reaches 80%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a PITI mortgage payment?

PITI stands for Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance. It represents the complete monthly housing payment collected by most mortgage loan servicers.

When is Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) required on a conventional mortgage?

PMI is required on conventional mortgages when the buyer puts down less than 20% down payment. It is typically removed once home equity reaches 20% to 22%.

How much can I save by choosing a 15-year mortgage over a 30-year mortgage?

A 15-year mortgage features slightly higher monthly payments but comes with a lower interest rate and cuts total interest paid over the life of the loan by more than 50%.
