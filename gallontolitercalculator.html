The Ultimate Guide to Gallon and Liter Conversions

Whether you are tracking the fuel efficiency of your car on an international road trip, maintaining the delicate chemical balance of a large aquarium, or following an old family recipe passed down from overseas, measuring liquids correctly is incredibly important. However, the world is divided: the United States clings to the traditional gallon, while the vast majority of the globe relies on the metric system's liter.

Why Are There Different Types of Gallons?

One of the biggest mistakes people make when converting volume is assuming that all gallons are identical. There are two primary standards:

  • The US Liquid Gallon (3.785 Liters): The standard unit used across the United States. Originally based on the old British wine gallon (231 cubic inches).
  • The UK Imperial Gallon (4.546 Liters): Created in 1824 in Great Britain, defined as the volume of 10 pounds of water at 62°F, making it roughly 20% larger than the US liquid gallon.

The Gallon to Liter Formula

To convert US liquid gallons to liters manually, multiply the number of gallons by 3.78541:

Liters = US Gallons × 3.785411784
Liters = Imperial Gallons × 4.54609

Worked Example

10 US gallons → 37.85 litres. 10 imperial gallons → 45.46 litres (20% more).

US Liquid Gallon (3.785 L) vs UK Imperial Gallon (4.546 L)

In contrast, 1 UK Imperial Gallon equals 4.54609 liters (~4.546 L), making an Imperial gallon approximately 20% larger in volume than a standard US gallon.

Exact Conversion Formulas & Reciprocal Liters to Gallons Math

To convert US gallons to liters, multiply the gallon value by 3.78541: Liters = Gallons * 3.78541. To convert liters back to US gallons, divide liters by 3.78541, or multiply by the reciprocal factor 0.264172: Gallons = Liters * 0.264172. For Imperial gallons, use the multiplier 4.54609 or reciprocal 0.219969.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many liters are there in 1 US liquid gallon?

One US liquid gallon equals exactly 3.785411784 liters (commonly rounded to 3.785 liters). To convert US gallons to liters, multiply the gallon value by 3.78541: Liters = Gallons * 3.78541.

What is the difference between a US gallon and a UK Imperial gallon?

A US liquid gallon is defined as 231 cubic inches (~3.785 liters), whereas a UK Imperial gallon is defined as 10 pounds of distilled water at 62°F (~4.54609 liters). An Imperial gallon is approximately 20% larger than a US liquid gallon.

How do you convert liters back into US gallons?

To convert liters back into US liquid gallons, divide the volume in liters by 3.78541, or multiply by 0.264172: Gallons = Liters / 3.78541. For example, 10 liters equals approximately 2.64 US gallons.

Where are gallons still commonly referenced in Indian trade and engineering?

While India adopted the metric system (liters) for retail fuel and commerce, gallons are still frequently encountered in imported machinery specifications, commercial aquarium volumes, water treatment capacities, and aviation fuel references.
