Bible Verse of the Day – Data & Account Deletion

Dedicated Deletion Portal • Life Hack Developer
App: Bible Verse of the Day | Developer: Life Hack Developer (Google Play Store)

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies (Account and Data Deletion Requirement), users of Bible Verse of the Day (published by Life Hack Developer) can request the complete and permanent deletion of their account profile, favorited verses, prayer notes, and associated data at any time without needing to reinstall the app.

What data we collect & store

To provide daily scripture notifications and cloud sync across your devices, the app may store:

  • Google Profile (Name & Email): Used exclusively for authentication via Google Sign-In (OAuth).
  • Favorited Verses & Bookmarks: Saved scripture verses, custom reflection notes, and prayer requests.
  • Reading Streaks & History: Daily devotional reading streak progress and notification scheduling preferences.
  • Settings: Preferred Bible translation (e.g., KJV, NIV), theme, and font sizing.
  • Anonymous Crash Telemetry: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic logs via Google Play Services / Crashlytics.

How to request deletion

You can request account and data removal through any of the following methods:

  1. Online Form (Recommended): Fill out and submit the dedicated request form below.
  2. Direct Developer Email: Email developerravin@gmail.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request - Bible Verse of the Day” from your registered account.
  3. In-App Deletion: Open the app > Settings > Account > tap “Delete Account & Data”.

What happens next & timeline

  • Your ticket will be prioritized and processed within 7 business days.
  • All personal credentials, OAuth tokens, and saved verses will be permanently purged from our production databases.
  • A final confirmation email will be delivered to your registered email address upon completion.

Data we may retain

After your account is deleted, we only retain strictly anonymous, non-identifiable telemetry (such as aggregated crash reports or anonymized download counts) that cannot be linked back to you or any individual device, solely for app stability.
